# Haven_Delhi_FInal_Version

INSTRUCTIONS

You walk down an infinitely long stairwell, where you must reach floor 100 in order to win

You are equipped with a flashlight which you turn on by pressing F

You can sprint by holding shift

There are 2 entities: a lethal one and a non-lethal one

No entities can spawn before floor 15. After that, the chances of any one entity spawning increases as you go deeper (5% to max 15%)

An entity spawns a few floors below you, and you see it coming your way down. The only way to survive is not to make eye contact with the entities (you look either upwards or sideways). Both the entities disappear after 5 seconds and both entites have a max eye contact time of 1.2 seconds

If you encounter the non-lethal entity and make eye contact, you will be jumpscared and sent 15 floors back

If you encounter the lethal entity, you will be jumpscared and killed (you start from floor 0)

PRO-TIP: You can phase through the entities by sprinting if you are fast enough
